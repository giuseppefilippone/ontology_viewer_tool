SWRL rules of the workspace, with fuzzy degrees (Fuzzy OWL 2 style: a degree annotation on the rule).

  • Rules render in human syntax (antecedent → consequent); + adds a rule through the guided builder (atoms: class, property, builtin), each rule can be removed.
  • Run applies the rules with the chosen engine and lists the derived assertions.
  • Export — CSV, LaTeX or PDF of the rule list.
